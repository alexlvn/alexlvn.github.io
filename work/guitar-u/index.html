Product

Guitar U helps 50,000+ musicians a month find tabs (lyrics with chords) for their favorite songs. Users browse a catalog of 236k songs and 22k artists, play along with auto-scroll, and save favorites for offline use. A "Guitar U Plus" subscription unlocks extra features, such as exporting a song with chords as a custom-generated PDF.

My role

A freelance client reached out to me to develop their idea of an app with lyrics and chords of songs for guitarists.

I developed the application end-to-end in 4 months, and maintained it for over a year. Acted as a Flutter Developer, Product Manager and UI/UX Designer: collected requirements, wrote exhaustive specification documents, designed the UI for every screen in Figma, made an interactive prototype, hired a backend engineer, and built the Flutter app.

The product reached 50,000+ MAU and 400,000+ installs and holds a catalog of 236k songs and 22k artists.

What I Built & Owned

  • Song discovery — catalog page holding 22k artists at a constant 60 FPS, global search, and artist pages with user ratings for each tab.
  • Feature-rich tab page — pin, filter, or resize chord diagrams; transpose a song to another key; view chord variations; player-like auto-scroll mode; left-handed mode.
  • Favorites — users can add artists and tabs to favorites, making them accessible when offline, handy for musicians bringing their instrument on a camping trip.
  • Playlists — users categorize songs by creating playlists, which are also fully available offline.
  • Top 100 & Recommended — most-viewed songs & recommended songs selected by moderators.
  • Social sign-in — Apple and Google accounts, driving 78,000+ registrations.
  • Web Admin Portal — a Flutter Web CMS where admins can create/edit/delete artists and tabs as well as export monthly tab-view reports to share with investors.
  • Automated testing — the app is fully covered with 237 UI tests that act like a real user, tapping through every feature before each release to catch bugs.

Also shipped: deep-link sharing, observability via Crashlytics, push notifications, analytics, localization.

Under the hood

The hardest part was keeping the music readable on a small screen. A chord sits above the exact word where the guitarist changes finger position. On a wide screen that's easy, but on a phone long lines have to wrap, and standard text rendering wraps the lyrics on their own, so a chord ends up above the wrong word. For a guitarist, that makes the song unplayable. I built a custom RenderObject that wraps the chord line and the lyric line together, keeping them aligned on every screen size, and reused the same layout logic in the PDF generator so exported songs look right on paper.
