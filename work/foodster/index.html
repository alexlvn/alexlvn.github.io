Product

Foodster is a cross-platform food delivery app partnered with 90+ restaurants across 3 cities, processing 30k+ orders/month. Users discover food, build a cart and place orders that are easy to track in the app or through push notifications — all within a single polished mobile experience on iOS and Android.

My role

The team brought me in to solve user complaints about the quality of the main customer-facing product: bugs and poor UI performance.

I developed a new Flutter app end-to-end in 4 months: introduced architectural standards and documentation, mentored engineers, polished UX in close collaboration with the designer, integrated secure bank payments with backend engineers, and consulted the business on aligning team processes.

Negative user reviews about the quality of the app disappeared. 6 months after release, mobile profit doubled. I owned the app for 14 months, through 97 releases.

What I Built & Owned

  • Food discovery — performant lists of restaurant and food items running at a consistent 60 FPS, with smooth gesture-driven animations and attention to detail. Plus Instagram-like stories, tag filtering, search, ad placements.
  • Cart & checkout — non-blocking UX, secure bank payments, promo codes, order status tracking, sophisticated adaptive layout and character animation.
  • Authentication & profile — 1-2-3 phone sign-in flow with SMS code auto-completion so users don't even have to think about it.
  • Address management — maps & geocoding integrated, with all the location-consent edge cases covered. Addresses are synced between devices.
  • Observability — error-tracking service integrated, health-monitoring processes set up and run regularly by the team.

Also shipped: push notifications, dual analytics (Firebase Analytics + AppMetrica), server-synced cart, localization, in-app surveys, ratings & feedback flow.

Cross-functional team: 3 Flutter developers, 2 Backend developers, 2 Frontend developers, Product manager, Designer, QA.

Tech stack: Flutter (bloc, rxdart, freezed, dart_mappable, fast_immutable_collections, go_router, localization, slivers, Lottie, push notifications, maps, geolocation, http, CI/CD, linter rules, DCM), UI Kit based on the design system, AppMetrica, Sentry, GitHub Copilot, AI code review.
