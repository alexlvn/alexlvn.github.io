What I Built & Owned

  • Day-by-day ordering flow — sophisticated home page layout helping to track statuses of multiple multi-day orders, all edge cases carefully thought out and handled.
  • Payments — secure card payments via Square, Apple Pay and Google Pay, with in-app bank card management. Taxes are handled, with support for tax-exempt companies.
  • Guest mode — lets users sign in as a "guest" and try the app without going through an invite-only sign-up process.
  • Weekly rotating menus — management can modify per-company menus without affecting the ones users currently see.
  • Fault-tolerant bulk payments — users only pay on the day of the order: every morning the backend automatically runs hundreds of transactions at once for everyone with an order that day.
  • Subsidy rules engine — companies split employees into groups (e.g. interns vs. seniors), each with its own rules: a fixed amount or a percentage off each item, set per day of the week.
  • Financial-grade credit system — companies can give their employees expiring credits that are used at checkout to further subsidize orders.

All the functionality above was brought to life by an extensive TypeScript + Firebase backend I developed, including server-enforced, role-based access for 6 user types.

Also shipped: social sign-in (Google, Apple, Facebook), push notifications, observability, analytics, localization.

Cross-functional team: 4 Flutter developers, Product Manager, Designer, Frontend developer.

Flutter stack: bloc, MVVM, rxdart, built_value, beamer, get_it, localization, Firebase, chopper, secure storage, custom UI Kit with theming, slivers, RenderObject, complex interactive animations, push notifications, CI/CD, linter rules, Crashlytics, social sign-in, square_in_app_payments.

Backend stack: TypeScript, Firebase, luxon, runtypes, ESLint & typescript-eslint, document-oriented databases, axios.
